## What the dashboard does

The dashboard helps a regional gym manager screen Ontario market zones for possible gym expansion. It combines demographic demand, existing gym supply, budget-gym availability, competitor ratings, amenities, and monthly fee comparisons.

## Main dashboard sections

1. **Filters** - select municipality, market zone, or search by gym/area.
2. **KPI cards** - summarize visible zones, population, gym records, budget-gap zones, and average opportunity score.
3. **Opportunity ranking** - shows which zones should be reviewed first.
4. **Demand vs. supply chart** - compares demand score against gyms per 10,000 people.
5. **Selected zone profile** - shows population, density, income, young adult share, renter share, total gyms, and budget competitors.
6. **Competitor locations in view** - lists visible gyms with brand, address, type, monthly price/amenities, training, rating/reviews, and verification notes.
7. **Pricing comparison** - shows monthly price gaps between visible competitors.
8. **Methodology / limitations** - explains scoring logic, data sources, AI-use disclosure, and verification limits.

## Important interpretation note

This is an academic screening dashboard. It should be used to shortlist areas for deeper review, not as a final real-estate decision tool. Final decisions would still require lease costs, traffic counts, parking, site visibility, local zoning, and updated competitor verification.
